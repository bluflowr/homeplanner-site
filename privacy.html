Privacy Policy

Homeplanner · last updated November 23, 2026

Homeplanner is a private app used by a single household. This page explains what it does with information from Google accounts that family members choose to connect.

What we access

  • Your email address, to show which Google account is connected.
  • Your Google Calendar events. The family calendar is read and updated so it can be shown and edited on the family's hub. A family member's personal calendar may be connected read-only so it can be displayed alongside it.

How it is used

  • Calendar events are copied into the household's own private database only to display them on the family's devices and to save changes family members make on the hub back to the same calendar.
  • Events marked private in Google Calendar are never shown on the shared hub.
  • Information is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • Access tokens are stored encrypted and are used only to keep the family's calendar in sync.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Homeplanner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Any connected account can be disconnected from the app's admin page, which deletes its stored tokens and cached events. You can also revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be raised by opening an issue on this site's GitHub repository.
